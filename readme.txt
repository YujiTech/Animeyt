#Anime Website

Thank you for using our template!
 
Anime is a free Bootstrap template for Gaming & Anime websites. To have access to all the fans of games and anime, nothing is more efficient than an online presence, and it gets extra wings when the Anime template adds to that. The web skin is 100% responsive, well-optimized, and compatible with all the available devices and retina screens. Anime is a contemporary, bold, and fully customizable page canvas you can use with your dream project.
